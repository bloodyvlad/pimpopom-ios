# PimPoPom app icon generation brief

- Date: 2026-07-15
- Tool: OpenAI image generation tool
- Reference images: None
- Intended use: Internal-development iOS app icon

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand

Asset type: production iOS app icon, 1024×1024 square

Primary request: Create an original icon for the fast color-reaction game “PimPoPom”, visually expressing three rhythmic beats or voices—Pim low, Po middle, Pom higher—as three bold tap targets ascending from lower-left to upper-right.

Scene/backdrop: full-bleed deep midnight navy background.

Subject: three large, simple circular reaction buttons or discs; left cyan (`#35E6DF`), middle pink (`#FF5BA8`), right yellow (`#FFD84D`); their vertical positions rise clearly and rhythmically. Each disc may have a restrained concentric highlight that suggests a tap pulse while remaining legible at very small sizes.

Style/medium: polished flat vector-like brand mark, playful, energetic, premium, with minimal dimensional lighting only where it preserves small-size clarity.

Composition/framing: centered strong silhouette, generous mask-safe margins, balanced negative space, unmistakable at 29 px.

Constraints: opaque square artwork; no transparency; no baked rounded-square corners; no text, letters, words, finger, hand, mascot, game-controller symbol, mockup, border, watermark, trademarked imagery, or extra objects; original design only; preserve the exact cyan, pink, yellow, and deep navy palette.

Avoid: photorealism, complex gradients, thin lines, tiny detail, external drop shadows, clutter.

## Export notes

The tool returned a 1254×1254 opaque PNG. The retained source is unchanged. The shipped asset-catalog copy was resized to 1024×1024 and tagged sRGB without adding a corner mask.
