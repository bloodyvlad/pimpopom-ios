# Audio sources

No audio file has been copied into or accepted by PimPoPom yet. Legacy audio may be evaluated only from the frozen migration baseline and copied later with its original provenance, licence, masters, generation scripts where applicable, and new PimPoPom hashes.

## Planned PimPoPom launch sting

Create an original short voice-like activation cue with three rising syllables:

1. **Pim** — low pitch;
2. **Po** — middle pitch;
3. **Pom** — higher pitch.

Requirements:

- Do not imitate an identifiable person's voice or a protected recording.
- If a human performs it, retain written performer consent/release and recording-session notes.
- If synthesized or generated, record the tool/model/version, full prompt or synthesis parameters, source inputs, and commercial-use terms applicable on the creation date.
- Retain a lossless production master and a reviewed runtime derivative. Record sample rate, bit depth/codec, channels, duration, integrated loudness, true/sample peak, leading/trailing silence, and SHA-256.
- The iOS system launch screen remains silent/static. Runtime playback begins only after the real app is active, never delays interaction, and follows the accepted Sound FX/Silent mode/background policy.
- Test on physical iPhone speakers, headphones, Bluetooth, Silent switch, interruption, and cold/warm launch.

## Migrated audio review

Before accepting any baseline theme tone bank, life-loss cue, or menu/gameplay loop:

- confirm original/third-party provenance and redistribution rights;
- retain lossless master or equivalent reproducible source;
- preserve click-free boundaries and exact cue slots where behavior depends on them;
- record original and new hashes without claiming bytes are identical unless verified;
- document `AVAudioEngine` mix gains separately from source mastering;
- name the first PimPoPom build and rollback commit.
