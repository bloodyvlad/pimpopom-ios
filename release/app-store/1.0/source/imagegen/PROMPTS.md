## Midnight neon

Generated source: `midnight-neon-original.png`

> Use case: ads-marketing
> Asset type: PimPoPom App Store launch hero background and marketing banner base
> Primary request: Create a premium, wide cinematic background for a fast colorful iOS reaction game called PimPoPom.
> Scene/backdrop: Deep midnight navy space with a subtle tactile game-surface texture. Bright cyan, emerald, yellow, coral-pink, violet, and electric blue light trails converge toward a central rhythmic burst. Include a few abstract softly rounded square light panels and tiny particles that suggest rapid tapping and reaction timing, but do not depict a real app interface.
> Style/medium: High-end 3D luminous digital art, polished Apple-quality mobile game campaign artwork, playful but not childish.
> Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape. Keep the left-center and center relatively calm for a real logo and exact marketing copy to be added later. Put the strongest color energy toward the right and outer edges. Strong depth, clean silhouette, no clutter.
> Lighting/mood: Additive neon glow, soft bloom, crisp highlights, energetic, optimistic, immediate.
> Color palette: Dark navy base with the exact-feeling PimPoPom rainbow sequence: green → yellow → orange → pink → purple → cyan.
> Materials/textures: Subtle glass, soft crystal diffusion, sparse pixel grain, no hard chrome.
> Constraints: background art only; no text, no letters, no logos, no app icon, no phone, no UI screenshot, no people, no animals, no coins, no watermark. Leave usable negative space for deterministic overlay.

## Light crystal

Generated source: `light-crystal-original.png`

> Use case: ads-marketing
> Asset type: wide 16:9 text-free marketing background for an iOS reaction game
> Primary request: Create an airy LIGHT / CRYSTAL visual world: a pale cyan and luminous white glass field with elegant rainbow crystal refractions and soft prismatic caustics. Include only a few abstract rounded-square reaction tiles, floating or embedded with tasteful depth, suggesting fast color-matching gameplay without showing any real interface.
> Style/medium: premium polished 3D illustration, sophisticated playful iOS game campaign art, clean glassmorphism, tactile crystal materials, crisp but gentle.
> Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape. Preserve a calm, uncluttered, low-detail negative-space area across the center-left for later exact logo and copy placement. Concentrate the richer crystal tiles and prismatic detail mostly toward the right edge and lower-right, with subtle framing detail in the far corners. No important object near the center-left.
> Lighting/mood: bright diffused daylight, soft translucent bloom, subtle rainbow dispersion, welcoming, optimistic, premium, energetic but not busy.
> Color palette: pale cyan, ice blue, white, transparent crystal, small controlled rainbow accents.
> Materials/textures: frosted glass, polished transparent crystal, softly beveled rounded squares, delicate light refraction; no harsh chrome.
> Text: none.
> Constraints: background only; generous safe area for overlaid brand copy; clean visual hierarchy; should crop gracefully for common web and social banners.
> Avoid: all text, letters, logos, phone or device frames, screenshots, UI panels, people, hands, faces, animals, pets, coins, currency symbols, watermarks, signatures, dark backgrounds, clutter, photorealistic rooms, recognizable third-party branding.
