Gameplay basics

Arcade

Tap the active cell matching Your Color. Mistakes cost lives, fast reactions fill the Speed Bar, and signed-in eligible runs can join the global leaderboard and earn progression.

Zen

Endless practice with no lives, deadline, ranked submission, or coin awards. Use End run when you want to stop.

Both modes can be played without signing in. If taps, sound, or animation seem wrong, first fully close and reopen the app, then check Settings for Glyphs, Sound FX, Music, and Haptics.

Profile and sign-in

Sign-in is optional for local play. A PimPoPom profile is required for shared players, public ranked scores, coins, achievements, paid themes, pets, and account-bound purchase credit. Open the person icon on the Main Menu to sign in, confirm a public nickname, sign out, or manage the account.

Use a nickname that does not reveal personal contact information. To report an inappropriate nickname, email [MODERATION EMAIL].

Arcade scores and the global leaderboard

Only eligible signed-in Arcade runs are submitted to the PimPoPom global leaderboard. The game service verifies the run protocol before ranking or rewarding it. Anonymous Arcade and all Zen play remain local and unranked.

If a result says it could not be saved, keep the app open, check your connection, and use Retry when offered. A valid but high-risk result can be saved while being withheld from public ranking for review.

Game Center

Game Center is optional and separate from the PimPoPom profile and global leaderboard. Open Profile → Game Center → Connect to let the app use your current Apple Game Center player. Choose Turn Off to stop PimPoPom from using it. Apple does not provide apps with a Game Center sign-out button; manage the Apple account itself in iOS Settings.

The current version does not publish PimPoPom scores or achievements to Game Center. Turning Game Center off does not disable local play, your PimPoPom profile, purchases, or the global PimPoPom Arcade leaderboard.

Coins, Remove Ads, and Restore Purchases

Prices and purchase confirmation come from Apple. Coin packs are consumable and are credited to the signed-in PimPoPom profile after server verification. Every valid coin pack also adds account-bound ad-free access while that purchase remains valid. The separate Remove Ads purchase is non-consumable, restorable, and may be Family-Shareable through Apple; it grants no coins.

Restore the standalone Remove Ads purchase

  1. Sign in to the intended PimPoPom profile.
  2. Open Remove Ads or Buy Coins.
  3. Choose Restore Purchases and wait for confirmation.
  4. If ads remain, close and reopen PimPoPom while online.

Consumable coin recovery comes from unfinished Apple transactions and the PimPoPom server ledger, not from Apple’s Restore Purchases list. For charge or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support. If Apple shows a completed purchase but PimPoPom did not credit it, contact us before buying again.

Advertising and Privacy Choices

Ad-supported players may see a bottom banner and an occasional interstitial after eligible completed runs. An account with a confirmed ad-free entitlement should show no ad control, banner, placeholder, or ad note.

Where available, reopen Google’s consent and privacy controls from Settings → Privacy Choices. Report an inappropriate or broken ad to [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the approximate time, country, placement, and a screenshot if safe. Do not tap an ad merely to identify it.

Delete your PimPoPom account

  1. Open the person icon on the Main Menu.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of My Profile and choose Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE MY ACCOUNT exactly.
  4. Complete recent sign-in verification if requested, then confirm deletion.

Deletion removes the PimPoPom identity, nickname, public results, coins, pets, themes, achievements, and active sessions. Limited pseudonymized purchase, refund, fraud-prevention, or settlement evidence may be retained as described in the Privacy Policy. Deletion does not delete your Apple, Game Center, or Google account and cannot be undone.

Troubleshooting

A score did not appear

Confirm you were signed in with a public nickname before starting Arcade, stayed online through Game Over, and did not play Zen. Retry a failed save when the app offers it.

A purchase is pending

Ask to Buy and bank approval can remain pending. Do not repurchase. Keep the same PimPoPom profile, reopen the app later, and let Apple finish the transaction.

Ads appear after purchase

Check that the purchase succeeded and the intended profile is signed in. Restore the standalone Remove Ads product, reconnect, and reopen the app.

Game Center opened unexpectedly

Open Profile and choose Turn Off. PimPoPom will stop initiating Game Center; manage Apple’s Game Center account in iOS Settings.
