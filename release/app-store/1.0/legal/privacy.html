This policy covers the PimPoPom iOS app, its player account and leaderboard service, and the support pages that link to this policy.

2. What we process

When you play without signing in

Arcade and Zen can be played locally without an account. The app stores settings such as selected theme, audio, effects, glyph, and accessibility preferences on your device. Anonymous play is not submitted to the global PimPoPom leaderboard and does not create an account-bound coin history.

When you create or use a PimPoPom profile

  • An internal random player identifier and your chosen public nickname.
  • A one-way digest that links the profile to the supported sign-in provider. We do not retain your provider password, raw provider subject, raw ID token, Google display name, or Google email address in the PimPoPom database.
  • Secure session records needed to keep you signed in and protect account actions.
  • Coins, achievements, pets, themes, purchase entitlements, and related account state.

Ranked Arcade play

Signed-in ranked Arcade runs may include chronological game events, reaction timings, derived scores, run identifiers, result status, rank, flags, and moderation evidence. The server uses this information to verify the game protocol, calculate rewards, prevent duplicate credit, operate the leaderboard, and investigate suspicious or broken results.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. PimPoPom activates Game Center only after you choose Connect in Profile. While connected, Apple may provide your Game Center display name and scoped player identifiers to the app. The current PimPoPom version keeps those values in memory for connection status and does not submit scores or achievements to Game Center. Choosing Turn Off stops PimPoPom from using Game Center; your Apple Game Center account is managed in iOS Settings.

Diagnostics, security, and support

Our service may process IP address, timestamps, request metadata, integrity and rate-limit signals, error records, and security logs. If you contact us, we process the information you choose to provide, such as your contact address, device/app version, public nickname, and problem description.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide Arcade and Zen gameplay and remember app settings;
  • create and secure profiles, sessions, progression, and public nicknames;
  • verify ranked runs, publish eligible scores, calculate ranks, and moderate abuse;
  • credit coins, complete cosmetic purchases, and maintain account entitlements;
  • verify App Store transactions, restore eligible purchases, and process refunds or reversals;
  • request and display advertising according to consent and ad-free status;
  • diagnose failures, protect the service, answer support, and comply with legal obligations.

Depending on where you live, these activities are based on your request to provide the service, your consent, our legitimate need to secure and improve the service, and obligations that apply to payments, refunds, safety, and accounting.

4. Public leaderboard and nickname

Your confirmed nickname and eligible Arcade score, rank, and related public game details can be visible to anyone who opens the global PimPoPom leaderboard. Do not put your real name, email address, phone number, or other sensitive information in your nickname. Results may be withheld or removed when they fail verification or moderation rules.

To report a nickname or leaderboard entry, contact [MODERATION EMAIL] and include the visible nickname and enough detail to identify the entry.

5. Service providers and disclosures

We use these categories of providers to operate PimPoPom:

  • Apple for app distribution, StoreKit purchases, refunds, optional Game Center, device operating services, and related fraud prevention.
  • Google for optional Google sign-in, Google Mobile Ads, and User Messaging Platform consent/privacy controls.
  • Hostinger and technical infrastructure providers for the PimPoPom API, database, security, and availability.
  • Professional advisers, regulators, or authorities where disclosure is required to protect users, enforce rights, process legal claims, or comply with law.

Providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies as well as our instructions where applicable. We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising disclosures can be treated as “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under some laws; see the choices below.

6. Advertising and privacy choices

Ad-supported accounts may receive banner and occasional interstitial ads through Google Mobile Ads. Before requesting ads, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform where required. Depending on your region, consent, device settings, and Google’s configuration, the ad services may process information such as IP address, coarse location derived from network information, device/app identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and performance data.

PimPoPom does not request App Tracking Transparency permission in the current version. You can reopen available Google privacy choices from Settings → Privacy Choices. You can also purchase an eligible ad-free entitlement. Declining an optional advertising choice does not block basic gameplay or purchases.

7. Purchases and paid-value records

Apple processes payment details; PimPoPom does not receive your full card number. To credit a purchase and prevent duplicate grants, we process Apple-signed transaction information, product and transaction identifiers, environment and bundle information, a server-generated account token, coin-lot and spend records, entitlement sources, and refund, reversal, revocation, or debt status.

Coin packs are consumable and are reconciled through your PimPoPom profile. The standalone Remove Ads product is restorable through Apple and may support Family Sharing where Apple makes it available. Refunds can remove related unspent value, entitlements, or cosmetics funded by the refunded transaction, according to the purchase rules shown in the app and Apple’s policies.

We retain active profile, progression, leaderboard, and transaction state while needed to provide your account. Security logs are generally retained for [SECURITY LOG RETENTION], and support records for [SUPPORT RECORD RETENTION], unless a longer period is reasonably needed for an open security, support, or legal matter.
